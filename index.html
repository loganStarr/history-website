Introduction

Justice for Janitors

Justice for Janitors was a labor movement run through the Service Employees International Union (SEIU). This organization fought for janitor rights, specifically better wages, benefits, and working conditions. The movement gained traction in the late 1980s and eventually became known for trying to challenge low wages, corporate power, and subcontracting. The SEIU connects to neoliberalism by resisting the labor market restructuring and the growth of low-wage contract workers.
